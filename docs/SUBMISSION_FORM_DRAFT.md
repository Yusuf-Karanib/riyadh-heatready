## Project title

Riyadh HeatReady

## One-line summary

Riyadh HeatReady ranks 1 km zones in southeastern Riyadh where confirmed 2018–2023 built-up growth overlaps hot 2025 land surfaces and modelled population. It combines Sentinel-1, Sentinel-2, annual land cover, Landsat, WorldPop, and open Planet Tanager data into a transparent field-inspection priority map for municipal planning and heat-resilience teams. The working PoC includes an executable notebook, dashboard, GIS-ready outputs, spatial holdout validation, and clear limitations.
